# Road_Safety_RTO - Docnish Road Safety & RTO Question Bank

Verified and corrected RTO Learner's Licence Question Bank dataset with updated traffic rules, road signs, and penalties under the **Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019**.

---

## 📌 Summary of Issues & Fixes Applied

The original question bank contained several critical errors, outdated legal penalties, and OCR scanning mistakes:

### 1. 🚨 Critical Wrong Answer Keys (गंभीर गलतियाँ)
- **Question ID 171 (PDF Q185) - Overtaking on Two-Lane Roads:**
  - *Original Issue:* The question bank incorrectly marked **Option 1 ("Pass the vehicle from the left hand side")** as correct.
  - *Correction:* Changed to **Option 2 ("Pass the vehicle from the right hand side")**. Under Regulation 23 of the Motor Vehicles (Driving) Regulations 2017 and Rule 14 of the Rules of the Road Regulations 1989, overtaking must always be conducted from the right. Overtaking on the left is illegal and hazardous.
- **Traffic Sign (PDF Q70) - Mandatory Turn Right Sign:**
  - *Original Issue:* Blue circular sign shows arrow pointing ahead and right, but answer key marked "Compulsory ahead or turn left".
  - *Correction:* Marked correctly as **"Compulsory ahead or turn right"**.
- **Traffic Sign (PDF Q48) - Traffic Prohibited in Both Directions:**
  - *Original Issue:* Sign shows two vertical arrows crossed out with red slash, marked as "One-way".
  - *Correction:* Marked correctly as **"Prohibited in both directions"**.

### 2. ⚖️ Outdated Penalties (Motor Vehicles Amendment Act 2019)
The original question bank referenced obsolete fines from before the 2019 Amendment:

| Violation | Question Bank (Old) | Updated Law (MV Amendment Act 2019) | Section |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seat Belt Violation (ID 416)** | ₹100/- | **₹1,000/-** | Section 194B |
| **Drunk Driving (ID 325 / Q161, Q347)** | ₹2,000/- / 6 months | **₹10,000/- / 6 months** (1st offence), **₹15,000/- / 2 years** (2nd offence) | Section 185 |
| **Overloading Goods Carriage (ID 177 / Q191)** | ₹2,000/- | **₹20,000/- + ₹2,000/- per extra tonne** | Section 194 |
| **Mobile Phone Usage (ID 386 / Q409)** | CMVR 21(25) / ₹100-500 | **₹1,000/- to ₹5,000/-** (Dangerous Driving) | Section 184(c) |
| **Driving Without Licence (Q424)** | ₹500/- | **₹5,000/-** | Section 181 |

---

### 3. 🚗 Speed Limits & Regulatory Updates
- **Car Speed Limit on National Highways (Q170):** MoRTH (Ministry of Road Transport and Highways) revised national speed limits to **100 km/h** on 4-lane divided National Highways and **120 km/h** on Expressways (Gazette Notification S.O. 1522(E), 2018).
- **Motorcycle Speed Limit (ID 160 / Q174):** National speed limit on highways is **80 km/h**.
- **PUCC Validity (ID 62 / Q67):** Under CMVR Rule 115(7), PUCC is valid for **1 year (12 months)** for all BS-IV and BS-VI vehicles (6 months applies only to pre-BS-IV vehicles).

---

### 4. 🔤 OCR & Typing Errors
- **ID 362:** Fixed OCR glitch where *"A school bus can be identified by... Creame yellow paint"* was mistakenly converted to *"Alcohol bus can be identified by... Cresine yellow paint"*.
